About This Thesis

This Master's thesis presents a deep learning approach to traffic flow prediction combining Graph Attention Networks with a two-branch temporal architecture. The model successfully predicts multi-scale traffic patterns over horizons ranging from 6 hours to 7 days, achieving high accuracy on real-world taxi data from New York City.
